Selected Work

Agentic root-cause analysis for distributed services Samsung Research India · 2025
Designed and built a multi-agent RCA prototype from literature review through evaluation — modality-specialist evidence collection, hypothesis validation, cross-incident retrieval memory, and diagnosis-only safety boundaries over Prometheus, Kubernetes, Jaeger, OpenSearch and Milvus, using Google ADK and MCP. Evaluated on an OpenTelemetry Demo + Chaos Mesh fault-injection testbed.
HOMA — multilingual agent orchestration for smart homes Samsung PRISM · ICCCNT 2025
Led a four-person research team building a multilingual SLM-orchestrated system that decomposes multi-device smart-home commands and coordinates sequential/concurrent execution across 11+ languages, with synthetic multilingual evaluation of Gemma/Qwen/Phi-family models under edge constraints.
code
HERO — EV charging-station placement via bandit search VIT Research · under review
A two-stage system for EV-charging-station placement: an XGBoost city-level demand model feeds a simulator-in-the-loop multi-armed-bandit search over full station layouts. Outperforms 13 facility-location, metaheuristic and bandit baselines by up to 39.9% composite reward in Ann Arbor SUMO microsimulation, with zero-shot cross-city demand transfer.
Multilingual audio deepfake detection testAIng · 2026
Owned ML systems and research for multilingual audio-deepfake detection (ECAPA-TDNN, WavLM, mixture-of-experts), reaching 0.94–0.96 AUC on internal multilingual benchmarks and 1.97% EER on Hindi fine-tuning, then migrated model-heavy inference to Docker/Kubernetes with HPA, probes and Prometheus/Grafana.
