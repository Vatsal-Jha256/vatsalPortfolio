I build machine learning systems that make decisions, and the infrastructure that runs them. I'm joining Samsung Research in Bangalore.

At Samsung Research I built a multi-agent system that diagnoses failures in Kubernetes services from metrics, traces and logs. At testAIng I trained multilingual audio-deepfake detectors and moved them to autoscaled Kubernetes inference ([writeup](https://medium.com/@vatsal.jha_28295/one-class-learning-for-audio-deepfake-detection-9a841b79a7bf)). At Samsung PRISM I co-led [HOMA](https://github.com/IISF-SIF/HOMA), small-model agents that carry out multilingual smart-home commands.

My research is on learning to decide under uncertainty, and on checking whether a model's predictions actually lead to good decisions. That includes client selection in federated learning ([BehaviorFL-MT](/publications/)), adaptive authentication ([CA-AMFA](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/abstract/document/11448177/), Best Paper at IEEE ICDSINC), and evaluation in carbon accounting and healthcare ([Ranking Is Not Valuation](/publications/), [AdaptiveCareArena](/publications/)). I do this with Dr. B. Ashok at VIT, where I finished a B.Tech in Computer Science in 2026.
