I'm a research engineer. I work on machine learning that has to decide with limited capacity: which patients to check, which answers to verify, which claims to audit, where to build chargers. I care whether the gains we report survive a fair comparison, and who ends up served when resources are short.

Three questions run through my [research](/research/):

- **Does the improvement survive a fair comparison?** Against simple baselines, at matched cost, with verdicts that can say "equivalent" or "inconclusive".
- **Who gets observed, verified and served when capacity is scarce?** In healthcare, mobility, climate and public services.
- **Do guarantees checked in isolation hold once AI acts inside a real system?** Agents running infrastructure or acting for people, and the infrastructure that runs models.

Previously at Samsung Research India, Samsung PRISM and testAIng. I work with Dr. B. Ashok at VIT, where I finished a B.Tech in Computer Science in 2026.
